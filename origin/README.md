## Two routes

**Axiomatic.** Posit 0. Posit S. Define + and * by recursion. Add
induction to pick which structure you meant.

**Mechanism.** Posit a wrap. Ask what it can distinguish. Successor
is co-propagate with 1. Addition is two windings. Multiplication is
iterate. Z/n is the wrap. Z is refuse-to-close.

`routes.py` checks agreement on successor `{0..8}` and on 81 pairs
for + and *. They agree. That is the result.

The mechanism route trades a larger stipulation for a smaller one.
It does not escape stipulation.

## Not ontology

A wrap is a listed period. A numeral is a reading of a winding
against that period. `5` at `Closure(3)` is `2`. The bare numeral
is a reification.

Peano Nat is another listed cut. The intruder shows the axioms
without induction do not name "the" naturals.

Neither route needs a floor under the wrap. Paid, unpaid, cited.
A green gate prices coverage. It does not install a metaphysics.
