# mechanism

The seed. Coffee-stirring taken at its word.

A mark moves. It loads. Speed is support over support-plus-demand.
Demand over theta splits the mark and the ledger holds. Outflow
takes a cheap route.

V, G, theta are not priors. They fall out if you refuse A=A on two
seeds that share a starting (x,L) and differ in eta.

```
PYTHONPATH=. python3 -m mechanism.verify
```
